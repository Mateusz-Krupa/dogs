## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Explain suitable loose-lead equipment
The loose-lead exercise SHALL recommend a comfortable fixed-length lead around 2 metres, adapted to safety and dog size, and a well-fitting non-restrictive harness. It SHALL explain that an unlocked retractable reel maintains tension and can reward pulling by releasing more length, making slack-lead learning less clear. It MUST NOT claim that a retractable lead is always tight when locked with slack or guarantee that equipment alone solves pulling.

#### Scenario: Preparing for practice
- **WHEN** the owner opens the description and first-step preparation in either language
- **THEN** they can identify the recommended equipment and why a retractable lead is not recommended for this exercise without consulting a website

### Requirement: Allow normal exploratory sniffing
Guidance SHALL distinguish loose-lead walking from heel and from a continuous training drill. It SHALL explain that sniffing is normal enrichment, allow safe exploratory pauses without first earning every pause, and permit safe sniffing access as an optional training reward.

#### Scenario: Relaxed dog investigates smells
- **WHEN** an owner reads the prolonged-sniffing troubleshooting card
- **THEN** they are advised to allow safe sniffing and use short practice stretches rather than punish sniffing, insist on constant eye contact, or withhold all exploration

### Requirement: Help the owner move on without coercion
Troubleshooting SHALL explain how to practise a single departure cue in an easy setting, reward voluntary attention and a few steps on a slack lead, and lower difficulty if the dog cannot respond. It MUST NOT recommend jerking, dragging, repeated commands or using food to lure a fearful dog toward a trigger.

#### Scenario: Dog struggles to leave an interesting smell
- **WHEN** the dog is otherwise comfortable but does not respond to the departure cue
- **THEN** guidance advises an easier/quieter context and a pause rather than stronger lead pressure or command repetition

### Requirement: Refusal to walk receives welfare-first triage
A separate refusal-to-walk card SHALL distinguish ordinary sniffing from a dog that freezes, repeatedly sits or will not move. It SHALL prioritise veterinary assessment for new, persistent or unexplained refusal, especially with pain, limping or lethargy; advise checking equipment comfort and conditions; and advise retreat/distance and qualified reward-based help for persistent fear. Possible pain SHALL short-circuit training advice rather than be treated as stubbornness.

#### Scenario: Sudden or painful refusal
- **WHEN** refusal is new, unexplained or accompanied by pain/limping/lethargy
- **THEN** guidance tells the owner to stop training and contact a veterinarian, not apply a DIY movement-training fix first

#### Scenario: Fearful stopping
- **WHEN** the dog freezes or avoids something frightening
- **THEN** guidance advises safe distance/retreat and no forced approach, with professional support for recurring difficulty

### Requirement: Preserve bilingual offline training contracts
All revised and added advice SHALL be bundled with equivalent PL/EN meaning using existing description/setup/note and symptom/why/fix structures. This change SHALL preserve exercise identity, all four step ordinals and success criteria, unrestricted content access, raw offline history, per-dog/exercise/environment progress, and existing session lifecycle. It MUST NOT alter five-rep verdict thresholds, cancellation handling, incomplete-set semantics or derived-only verdicts.

#### Scenario: Content update with an interrupted session
- **WHEN** updated bundled guidance is used offline with existing progress or an unfinished set
- **THEN** advice remains available in both languages without network access or a new unlock and no migration, reset or change of training criteria is introduced

#### Scenario: Bilingual content review
- **WHEN** reviewers compare the equipment, sniffing, moving-on and refusal advice
- **THEN** both languages contain the same actionable safeguards and neither retains claims that one pulling success lasts forever, all sniff-free walks are punishment, or boring walks are the required solution
