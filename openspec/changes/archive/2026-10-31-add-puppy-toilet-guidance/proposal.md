## Why

Owners need a clear answer when a puppy starts urinating indoors, followed by a practical explanation of how outdoor toileting is learned. The existing puppy hub has four short foundations but no toilet-training guidance; this topic warrants a more detailed, readable guide rather than a scored exercise or a brief problem card.

## What Changes

- Add one freely accessible fifth puppy foundation, `puppy-toilet`, titled “Nauka czystości — sikanie w domu” / “Toilet training — indoor accidents”, after the existing four entries.
- Lead with medical stop-lines and a quick distinction: signs before toileting → calmly go outside; already toileting → let the dog finish without startling or forcibly interrupting; discovered afterwards → quietly clean up, never punish.
- Explain outdoor learning, reward timing, signals, realistic outing opportunities, unsuccessful outings, gradual independence, nights/absence, temporary indoor pads, cleaning and when professional help takes over. Use short titled sections, not a wall of text.
- Reuse existing puppy routes, disclaimer, evidence and write-free educational semantics. Add only an optional titled-section extension to puppy content; leave current guides and their copy intact.
- Require verified field-level source mapping, aligned PL/EN drafts, independent welfare review and user copy approval before bundling.
- Non-goals: toilet logs, reminders, schedules generated by the app, scores, completion, new behaviour cards, new illustrations, diagnosis/treatment, DB changes, cloud services, or expansion of the other guides.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `puppy-toilet-guidance`: Detailed, welfare-compatible responses to indoor accidents and gradual outdoor toilet learning, including readable structured sections and medical boundaries.

### Modified Capabilities
- `puppy-start-guidance`: Increase foundations from four to five without reordering existing entries; extend foundation content with optional bilingual sections and apply existing publication/evidence requirements to the new guide.

## Impact

Native SwiftUI only: puppy model/validator/view, manifest, new bundled JSON, Xcode resource membership, existing content/checksum/unit/UI tests and any necessary PL/EN UI keys. No domain, repository, migration, session or recommendation changes; five-rep verdicts and dog/exercise/environment progress remain unchanged.
