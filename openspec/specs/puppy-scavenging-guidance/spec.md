## Requirements
### Requirement: Puppy foundation teaches floor items without fear
The existing `puppy-first-exercises` foundation SHALL carry bilingual guidance for a dog that picks up and eats things from the floor — dropped food at home and found items outdoors. It SHALL treat mouth exploration as normal, SHALL keep basic management ahead of training (clear access to food, supervise during cooking, store hazards), and SHALL NOT instruct the owner to punish, chase or forcibly prise items from the mouth.

#### Scenario: Dropped food at home
- **WHEN** an owner opens the foundation in either language after something edible fell
- **THEN** the advice covers calmly removing the item, managing access while cooking, giving the dog its own legal activities, and practising with safe dog-sized props only — never medication, rubbish, sharp objects or toxic food

### Requirement: Leave and drop stay separate skills
Guidance SHALL distinguish telling the dog not to pick an item up (`leave`) from getting rid of something already held (`drop`), SHALL describe trading for something better with payment from the owner's own hand, and SHALL link to the existing `leave` and `drop` exercises without duplicating their scored steps, criteria or progression.

#### Scenario: Item already in the mouth
- **WHEN** the dog already holds something
- **THEN** the guide directs the owner to a practised voluntary trade rather than a struggle, and links to the canonical `drop` exercise while leaving that exercise's steps and verdict rules untouched

### Requirement: Skill difficulty is set by the setting
Guidance SHALL state that performance with a floor item at home does not transfer automatically to the street, SHALL direct owners to start in a quieter/easier setting with distance and to increase temptation only while the dog succeeds, and SHALL NOT recommend constant eye contact, lead-yanking, cue repetition or food-luring toward a feared stimulus.

#### Scenario: Street finds are harder than kitchen practice
- **WHEN** an owner reports the dog ignores the cue outdoors
- **THEN** the advice lowers environmental difficulty instead of escalating lead pressure, and safe sniffing and exploration remain permitted

### Requirement: Ingestion and guarding escalate before training
The foundation SHALL present referral lines ahead of actionable practice: suspected ingestion of something potentially toxic or obstructing (medication, poison, raw yeast dough, cooked bones, batteries, string) or a choking/obstruction suspicion calls for immediate veterinary contact rather than a training protocol; repeated ingestion of non-food items or a sudden change in an otherwise easy dog calls for veterinary assessment; growling, stiffening or snapping over an item, or escalating guarding, calls for veterinary/qualified behavioural help and no self-test of the dog. Practice toys are returned after a trade; a dangerous find never is.

#### Scenario: Toxic find
- **WHEN** the dog has swallowed something potentially toxic or a sharp/obstructing object
- **THEN** the guide sends the owner to a veterinarian immediately and offers no home remedy or continued practice protocol in that branch

#### Scenario: Guarding over a find
- **WHEN** the dog guards, freezes over or threatens over a stolen item
- **THEN** the guide forbids testing or punishing the dog and routes to professional help, consistent with the existing urgent block

### Requirement: Extension keeps the puppy contract intact
The extension SHALL keep exactly four puppy foundations with their stable IDs, SHALL preserve unrelated guides byte-for-byte, SHALL carry 1–5 HTTPS sources with a real re-verification date covering every touched claim, and SHALL perform no training writes when read. Exercise links for never-opened exercises SHALL keep ordinary lazy initialization and SHALL not modify existing progress, mastery, choices or partial sets. Five-rep verdicts, cancellation and incomplete-set semantics, derived verdicts, offline availability and per-dog/exercise/environment isolation SHALL be unchanged, and no age or prerequisite gate SHALL hide the advice.

#### Scenario: Reading the extension offline
- **WHEN** an owner opens the extended foundation offline in either language with existing progress and an unfinished set
- **THEN** all advice and citations render without network access, no guide-owned session or rep is recorded, and the only permitted write is the standard lazy initial state for an exercise opened for the first time

#### Scenario: Bilingual editorial review
- **WHEN** reviewers compare the touched PL and EN fields against the change's ledger
- **THEN** every claim has a field-level source mapping, both languages carry the same escalation order and safeguards, unrelated approved sentences are preserved, and no language claims professional endorsement of the app
